## What v3 adds over v2.1

| Feature | What it does | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paid keepers | Whoever settles a repaid advance or writes off an unpaid one is credited 10% of its fee, claimable (`claimCredit`) | The repayment side no longer depends on the team's operator |
| Execution fee (v3.1) | A fixed per-rollup fee the exiting user pays, held out of provider assets until settlement, then credited to the keeper | The on-chain test showed 10% of a 0.3% fee (0.0006 QOR on 2 QOR) cannot pay a keeper's gas (about 0.05 QOR); a fixed fee makes keeping worth it at any exit size, at no cost to providers |
| Utilisation fee | The lane's base fee up to a kink in pool utilisation, then rising in a straight line to a capped maximum (at most 10%) | Liquidity cannot be drained cheaply in a rush |
| Exposure caps | A largest single exit and a most-owed-at-once per rollup | One exit cannot take a batch's whole cover; one rollup cannot tie up the pool |
| Equivocation slashing | Two ML-DSA-87 signatures by one watcher over different results for one batch, both checked on chain, take its free stake (half to the reporter), remove it, and freeze the batch; works from off-chain signatures alone | Signing two stories is provable and now costs the stake |
| Emergency pause | Either admin alone, with one ML-DSA-87 signature, stops new exits only; settling, writing off, releasing, sweeping and provider withdrawals keep working; resuming needs both admins | A brake that cannot be used to trap anyone's money |
| `quote()` | Fee, payout, cover room and a status code (OK, PAUSED, NOT_CONFIRMED, CONTESTED, TOO_LATE, EXIT_TOO_LARGE, LANE_FULL, COVER, LIQUIDITY, TOO_SMALL) before the user signs | Wallets can explain an exit before it is sent |
| Watcher stats | Attestations, confirmations, equivocations, defaults and slashed amounts per watcher, on chain | The board can show each watcher's record |

Tests: 62 Foundry tests (41 from v2.1, 21 for v3), and 7 invariants over random sequences of every action, including keeper claims, equivocation reports and pausing: advances within cover, providers never lose (stricter than v2.1: the pool's own assets, after every watcher, keeper and reporter credit, cover all deposits), cash covers credits, locked stake within stake, per-rollup totals match, the exposure cap holds, held execution fees are covered. A deep run of 1,000 sequences of 128 random actions (128,000 calls, 7 min 16 s) held all seven.
