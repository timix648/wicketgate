# Limits

What Wicketgate does not do, or does not do yet. Kept current; dates are UTC. Last reviewed 20 November 2026, against pool v3.1.

## Open

- **Testnet only.** The `pqcVerify` and `pqcKeyStatus` precompiles are inactive on QoreChain mainnet, so the contracts fail closed there: no attestation, admin action or watcher join can pass. Every post-quantum check goes through one library (`contracts/src/lib/PQGuard.sol`), so nothing else changes when the precompiles go live.
- **Testnet economics.** On pool v3.1 the fee is 0.3% up to 50% utilisation, rising to at most 3%; the quorum is 2; the keeper fee is 0.08 QOR per exit; the largest exit is 50 QOR on `wicketgate-pay` (20 on the Vigil rollups, 8 on the organiser's) and at most 160 QOR may be owed by one rollup at once. W1 and W2 stake 300 QOR each on v3.1. Each confirming watcher locks 50 QOR per batch advanced against on `wicketgate-pay`, 20 QOR on `vigil-1` and `vigil-pay-1`, and 8 QOR on `rdk-v328-e2e-mwd5v89d`. W3 (the drill account) stays staked with 50 QOR in the v2.1 registry for the drill challenge. None of these numbers is tuned for real value.
- **The watchers that sign today are ours.** W1, W2 and W5 are run by the team, so a quorum of 2 is the team vouching for a batch with its own stake. Since 22 November they run two independent reader implementations (W1 and W2 use the RDK reader, W5 recovers each post by its committed hash), so one reader bug cannot blind all three, and with three watchers a quorum of 2 survives one watcher being down. The design does not depend on the team: anyone with a proven ML-DSA-87 key and stake can join with one command (`onboard-watcher.ts`, see docs/WATCHERS.md), and one dissenting watcher freezes payouts on a batch. No outside operator has joined yet.
- **Not audited.** Pool v3.1 has 62 Foundry tests and 7 invariants over random action sequences (a deep run: 1,000 runs of 128 random actions, 128,000 calls, every invariant held), on-chain probes, and a red team of 18 attacks replayed against pool v2.1 ([docs/REDTEAM.md](docs/REDTEAM.md)). No external audit.
- **The pool cannot see rollup state.** The EVM cannot read `x/rdk`, so the pool learns that a batch was wrong only when an advanced exit address is not funded by its due time; then the confirming watchers' locked stake repays the pool. Watchers who confirm a wrong batch that nobody exits against are not slashed by the pool (the submitter still is, by the chain's fraud proof). A read-only precompile for RDK batch status would close this; see "What we learned" in the README.
- **Withdrawals to 32-byte addresses** cannot be exited instantly; they are left out of the exit tree and take the normal path.
- **The relay is run by the team.** It cannot forge or alter users' transactions, and users can always post to a based rollup's inbox themselves, but the convenience path depends on the team's server being up.
- **Notes hide content only** (see THREAT_MODEL.md, section 7).
